About SMARTTEP ACADEMY

SMARTTEP ACADEMY is a place that turns complete beginners into professionals, able and ready for work.

We advocate teamwork, transparency, and oneness. Our learning environment is designed around practical learning, structured courses, assignments, assessments, instructor guidance, and tools that help learners build useful skills.

What learners can do

Learn

Access course lessons, notes, audio and video resources, assignments, and practical learning activities.

Practice

Use exercises and the browser-based coding sandbox to turn concepts into practical work.

Assess

Complete attendance, exams, objective questions, theory questions, and other academic activities.

Build

Develop practical skills through projects and guided learning that can prepare learners for work.

Instructors

Approved instructors can create courses, lessons, assignments, and exams through the instructor portal. Instructor applications are reviewed by SMARTTEP ACADEMY administrators, and official instructor identities can be verified publicly.

Student verification and certificates

SMARTTEP ACADEMY provides public verification tools for official student and instructor records and supports certificate-request workflows through the platform.

Our community standard

Discrimination, gender inequality, sexual or sex-related talk, and abusive words are prohibited in the SMARTTEP ACADEMY community. Users are expected to communicate respectfully and use the platform for legitimate educational purposes.

Advertising and sustainability

SMARTTEP ACADEMY may display clearly identified advertisements or sponsored banners to help support the operation of the platform. Advertising is kept separate from core academic decisions and users are never required to click advertisements to access legitimate learning services.
