Smart monitors instructor activity for repeated denied requests, unusual request volume and excessive destructive actions. Critical patterns can automatically suspend an instructor; you can review, resolve, suspend or restore access here.

Instructor applications & accounts

Approve users who want to teach on WPS Academy. Approved instructors get their own portal. You can suspend or restore instructor access at any time.

Students & identity records

Manage student access and the official identity details used for WPS Academy student ID cards and public verification. You can assign or update the WPS Student ID, course code, registration/end dates, state, country and gender.

Course management

Create, edit, lock/unlock, or permanently delete courses. Deleting a course also removes its lessons, assignments, submissions, progress records, and student enrollment references.

Upload / update lesson media

Create a lesson with any combination of note, video and audio. You can also select an existing lesson and add or replace just one media file later. Uploading note, audio and video separately will update the same lesson instead of creating duplicates when the course and title match.
