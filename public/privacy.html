Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

SMARTTEP ACADEMY respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or use the SMARTTEP ACADEMY website and learning platform, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the platform, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account credentials in protected form, student or instructor identification details, course enrolments, learning progress, attendance, assignments, exam attempts, certificate requests, instructor applications, payment-submission information, and messages or files you choose to submit.

2. Technical and security information

To operate and protect the platform, we may process technical information such as IP address, browser and device information, session information, request activity, security events, and account activity timestamps. SMARTTEP ACADEMY uses this information for authentication, fraud and abuse prevention, security monitoring, troubleshooting, and platform integrity.

3. Cookies and similar technologies

SMARTTEP ACADEMY uses cookies and related technologies for essential functions such as maintaining secure login sessions and remembering necessary application state. Where advertising is enabled, advertising partners may also use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertising performance and deliver or personalize ads according to their own policies and applicable settings.

4. Google AdSense and advertising

If Google AdSense is enabled and the site has been approved to serve Google ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect information associated with visits to pages containing advertisements, including information used to measure ad delivery and effectiveness. Google may use advertising cookies for personalized or non-personalized advertising depending on the applicable settings, consent requirements, and configuration. SMARTTEP ACADEMY does not control Google's independent processing of information; users should review Google's advertising and privacy information for current details.

SMARTTEP ACADEMY may also display direct advertisements sold to advertisers. Direct advertising links are clearly identified as advertisements or sponsored content where appropriate.

5. How we use information

Information may be used to provide courses and services, authenticate accounts, process enrolments and payments, manage exams and certificates, communicate with users, support instructors, improve the platform, enforce community rules, investigate security events, prevent abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. AI features

SMARTTEP ACADEMY may provide AI-powered tutoring and learning tools. Information submitted to an AI feature is processed to provide the requested learning function and may be subject to the privacy terms of the underlying AI provider. Do not submit information to AI tools that you do not have permission to share.

7. Uploaded files and learning materials

Files submitted for assignments, instructor applications, chat attachments, lessons, payments, or other platform features may be stored with service providers used to operate the platform. Access is controlled according to the relevant account role and feature.

8. Sharing and service providers

We may use trusted technology providers for hosting, databases, media storage, authentication/session storage, AI services, payment-related processing, and advertising. Information is shared with a provider only as reasonably necessary to operate the requested service, maintain security, or comply with applicable requirements.

9. Data security

SMARTTEP ACADEMY uses security controls such as protected sessions, password hashing, access controls, rate limiting, security monitoring, upload screening, and administrator security controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Data retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, account and academic records, security, dispute handling, legal requirements, or legitimate operational needs.

11. Your choices and requests

You may request access to or correction of personal information associated with your account, subject to identity verification and applicable law. You may also request assistance with account or privacy questions through the public contact page or the support channel provided by SMARTTEP ACADEMY.

12. Children's privacy

SMARTTEP ACADEMY is an educational platform. Users should provide information only with the authority required by applicable law. Where a child or young learner uses the service, a parent, guardian, school, or other authorized adult should supervise use where required.

13. Third-party links and services

The platform may link to or integrate with third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the platform, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

15. Contact

For privacy or data questions, use the SMARTTEP ACADEMY Contact page.
