Recording guidance

After a class, the instructor can upload an MP4, WebM or MOV recording (maximum 100 MB per upload). Students enrolled in that course can then watch it from this page. For larger recordings, compress or split the video before uploading.

External services may provide their own recording features and restrictions. The instructor is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent before recording students.
