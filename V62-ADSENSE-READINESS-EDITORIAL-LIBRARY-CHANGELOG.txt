SMARTTEP ACADEMY V62 — ORIGINAL EDITORIAL LIBRARY & PUBLIC ARTICLE SEO

BASE
- Built directly from the V61 Practical Assessment project.
- Existing V61 features and existing admin/instructor article publishing routes are retained.

NEW EDITORIAL CONTENT
- Adds 19 original, beginner-focused educational articles (approximately 6,900 words total).
- Topics include Artificial Intelligence, Web Development, Cybersecurity, Python, JavaScript, CSS, React, TypeScript, Vue, App Development, Data Science, Django, Encoding/Decoding, C#, Git/GitHub, Databases, SEO, AI research literacy and Software Testing.
- Articles are seeded idempotently into the existing MongoDB articles collection at startup; stable seed keys prevent duplicate insertion on later restarts.
- Seeded articles are published as SMARTTEP ACADEMY Editorial Desk content and remain manageable by Admin.
- Articles are associated with matching available courses. If a matching course is not present at startup, that article is skipped rather than assigned to an unrelated course. The seed runs on a later restart when matching courses exist.

PUBLIC LIBRARY / DISCOVERY
- Reworked public Learning Library layout with search, course filter, topic tags, reading time and responsive article cards.
- Adds stable public article URLs at /articles/:slug.
- Adds server-rendered article pages with unique title, description, canonical URL and Open Graph metadata.
- Sitemap now includes the Learning Library and published article URLs with last-modified dates.
- Existing /article.html?id=... endpoint and API remain for backward compatibility.

PUBLISHING PERMISSIONS
- Admin retains the ability to create, edit and delete any article.
- Instructors retain the ability to create, edit and delete their own articles, subject to existing course ownership checks.
- Existing draft/published workflow is retained.
- No new external service, package, environment variable, account or paid subscription is introduced.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This upgrade does not guarantee Google AdSense approval, search ranking or indexing.
- Seed insertion requires MongoDB to connect and matching available course records to exist.
- No live Render, MongoDB Atlas, Cloudinary, browser crawler or AdSense review was performed.
- Articles should be reviewed by the academy before relying on them as formal course material; update examples and technical guidance as technologies change.
